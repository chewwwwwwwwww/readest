# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Brandon reads and listens across Mac, iPhone and iPad. He wants his own library,
reading progress and annotations, with offline speech downloads on his own build.

## Product Purpose

A personal Readest fork with calmer Readwise-Reader-inspired presentation. Keep
upstream's working reading and speech engines and self-hosting contracts.
Success is opening a book, reading comfortably, and finding offline audio quickly.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, reader-first. Content leads and chrome recedes.

## Anti-references

Cute mascots belong to other projects, not bookrack. Avoid crowded desktop-era
chrome, ornamental card stacks, oversized pill controls and competing accents.

## Design Principles

- Preserve familiar reading and library interactions.
- Give reading text room and keep metadata secondary.
- Make offline audio discoverable without changing entitlement or engine logic.
- Keep the fork delta small, token-driven and easy to reconcile with upstream.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve upstream keyboard labels, focus behavior, e-ink support, custom themes,
RTL and safe areas. Aim for WCAG AA text contrast, 44px touch targets and reduced
motion. No new accessibility requirements beyond upstream are asserted.
